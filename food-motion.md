# food motion

## Goal
Translate ingredient references into achievable web choreography.

## Workflow
1. Use ingredient burst, pasta spiral and plated garden as stylized studies. The uploaded food reference suggests falling, spiraling, separating and converging ingredients; it does not prove a real-time fluid renderer.

2. For photoreal liquids or complex food deformation, prefer a licensed prerendered video with poster and accessible controls, or approved offline asset production. Use lightweight geometry for interactive previews.

3. Design a beginning, focal hold and settle. Use a few meaningful ingredients rather than hundreds of heavy meshes. Keep nutrition/product claims grounded in supplied content.

## Acceptance / stop condition
Disclose stylized versus photoreal output; provide a static image/SVG fallback and a motion-off experience.

## Evidence to retain
Relevant file paths, exact commands actually run, observed outcomes and unresolved limitations. Ask before destructive changes or external paid operations.
