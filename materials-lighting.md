# materials lighting

## Goal
Establish studio-like depth with physically plausible materials and restrained lighting.

## Workflow
1. Use a key, fill and rim strategy. The supplied renderer generates a small RoomEnvironment for reflections; external HDRIs are optional licensed upgrades.

2. Metals need environment reflection and plausible roughness. Matte food needs higher roughness than glossy glaze. Clearcoat is not the same as transparent glass. Do not call a procedural opaque starter a fluid simulation.

3. Choose exposure and tone mapping before tuning colors. Check clipped highlights, black crushed shadows and unreadable labels. Share materials where possible and dispose textures.

## Acceptance / stop condition
The silhouette reads against its background at mobile size and highlights do not wash out product information.

## Evidence to retain
Relevant file paths, exact commands actually run, observed outcomes and unresolved limitations. Ask before destructive changes or external paid operations.
