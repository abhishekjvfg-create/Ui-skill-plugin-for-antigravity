# art direction

## Goal
Translate a simple brief into a distinct, coherent visual concept.

## Workflow
1. Extract audience, promise, primary action and brand constraints. Identify the one thing the visitor must understand in five seconds. Describe the concept in one sentence before choosing effects.

2. Compare two directions using typography, grid, material, light and movement. Select one and explain why it supports the content. Use the references for rhythm and composition, never to copy logos or claim ownership of an existing site.

3. Produce a direction note, hierarchy sketch and content outline. Reject generic filler, fake statistics, unsupported endorsements and decorative animation that obscures the message.

## Acceptance / stop condition
The user can identify the product, benefit and next action without waiting for animation.

## Evidence to retain
Relevant file paths, exact commands actually run, observed outcomes and unresolved limitations. Ask before destructive changes or external paid operations.
