# asset licensing

## Goal
Preserve lawful provenance for every visual and font asset.

## Workflow
1. Use user-owned assets, appropriately licensed assets or explicitly approved original generation. Reference videos are inspiration, not permission to redistribute their brands, labels, models or music.

2. Font catalog entries are names/metadata and upstream loading URLs. Check each family license before redistribution or self-hosting and keep required notices. Do not bundle commercial font binaries.

3. Track origin URL, author, license, retrieval date and modifications. Avoid images with commercial stock watermarks/credits unless rights are supplied. Treat generated assets as subject to provider terms.

## Acceptance / stop condition
Delivery contains asset provenance and no unsupported ownership or licensing claims.

## Evidence to retain
Relevant file paths, exact commands actually run, observed outcomes and unresolved limitations. Ask before destructive changes or external paid operations.
