# product scenes

## Goal
Compose readable 3D product storytelling instead of hiding commerce in a canvas.

## Workflow
1. Select a silhouette and view that make the product identifiable. Use the can, bottle, carousel or helix scene as a starting point. Procedural objects need replacement by licensed product GLBs for exact realism.

2. Keep camera and text choreography deliberate. Use HTML buttons for selection, a stable product heading and a factual details area. Raycasting can supplement but must not be the only way to interact.

3. Balance material roughness, environment reflection and rim lighting. Avoid exaggerated bloom and glass transmission on mobile. Do not imply sample product properties are factual.

## Acceptance / stop condition
Product details and next action are available to keyboard and screen-reader users independently of the scene.

## Evidence to retain
Relevant file paths, exact commands actually run, observed outcomes and unresolved limitations. Ask before destructive changes or external paid operations.
